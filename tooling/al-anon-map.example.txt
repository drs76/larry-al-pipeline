# al-anon-map.example.txt — name→placeholder map for anonymise.py
#
# Copy this to your LOCAL, un-committed map (default:
# /mnt/rojaws/localDev/al_merge/al-anon-map.txt, or set $AL_ANON_MAP) and fill in
# your REAL names. This example ships NO real names.
#
# Format: one rule per line,  regex<TAB>replacement   (a literal TAB between them).
# Blank lines and #-comments are ignored. Rules apply IN ORDER, case-insensitive,
# global. Put longer / more specific patterns first. Use \b for short ambiguous
# tokens (e.g. \bABC\b so it doesn't match inside another word).
#
# GUIDs, /mnt/c/users/<name> paths, and the host are redacted automatically by
# anonymise.py — you do NOT need rules for those here.

# --- company / employer ---
My Company Ltd	PerTenantExtension
My Company	PerTenantExtension
MYCO	PTE

# --- people ---
Firstname[._ ]?Lastname	<user>
\bFirstname\b	<user>

# --- customers (generic placeholders) ---
Big Customer Name	ClientA
\bBCN\b	ClientA

# --- vendors / ISVs ---
Some Vendor Ltd	VendorX
Some ISV	ISV1
